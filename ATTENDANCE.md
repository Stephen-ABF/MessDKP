# Attendance captures and cloned ticks

Attendance is an officer workspace area. Attendance read access opens raid nights and individual source captures; attendance write access is required to upload or clone a dump. An account does not need Overview access just to take attendance.

## When a capture was missed

Open the raid night, choose the existing capture whose roster should be reused, and select **Clone dump**. Enter the intended attendance time and explain why the capture is being copied. Review the roster count and raid DKP rate, then explicitly confirm the new tick. A clone is an officer reconstruction, not evidence that a new game export was taken.

The clone stays within that raid night. It reuses only the original exported roster, not characters added later through missing-attendance corrections. The registry remains authoritative for main/alt status and class. As with a normal import, approved mains receive the raid tick rate, unclaimed characters retain pending attendance and alts earn no main DKP.

The original capture and its raw text remain intact. Cloned ticks retain the source capture, target time, officer and reason. Clones are labeled in attendance views and recorded in the audit log. Identical retries return the existing tick rather than posting credit again; occupied capture times are rejected for review.

Cloning adds an attendance opportunity and may affect attendance percentages for other eligible guild members, just as adding a real tick does. Use it only when the officer intends that roster to count for the missing capture. No loot, bid or award record is created.

## Review and corrections

Click any source capture to see attendance for that tick. Members can request missing attendance for their own approved main; officers review those requests separately. Keep the original uploaded filenames. Persona classes in later dumps do not overwrite a character's established registry class.

Attendance percentages begin at the character's earliest evidenced registry/capture membership, not the guild's first recorded raid. All new and cloned attendance remains subject to the current access profile and the normal main-only earning rules.
