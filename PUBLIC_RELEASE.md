# Guild Desk 0.12.1

Replaces Min. bid with Your Bid in the compact Guild auctions list. Shows only the signed-in member's recorded bid amount; rows with no bid are completely blank, including no mobile label or DKP unit. Closed auctions retain your final bid when present; cancelled auctions are blank. The auction minimum remains in bidding details and publication controls.

Includes the0.12 compact layout, larger headings, matching item icons, copy counts, compact countdown and officer Controls pill/dialog, plus all0.11 wish-list imports, automatic filters, own bid totals/highlights and weapon ratios. No bid policy, reservation, pricing, DKP or schema change. No new migration after0015.

Preserve data,companion/state,original Python/settings and custom GUILD_DESK_DB; back up the stopped database before upgrading. Owner preview and live databases remain untouched. Test results accompany the ZIP; browser checks use disposable data. No hosting deployment or GitHubtag/Release is claimed.
